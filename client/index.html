Privacy Policy

DIU Nexus respects your privacy. This is a private campus network for DIU students only.

  • Your email, department & batch are used only for verification and campus features.
  • Posts marked Public are visible to all logged-in DIU members; Friends posts only to friends.
  • Messages are end-to-end between participants — not shared publicly.
  • We do not sell your data. Reports are reviewed only by Nexus Team Admins.
  • You can delete your posts or request account deletion anytime via Settings.

Last updated: 2026 • Contact: nexus@diu.edu.bd

Terms of Service

  • Use your official @diu.edu.bd email only. Fake accounts will be removed.
  • Be respectful — no harassment, hate, spam or false information. Violations may lead to warn/ban.
  • Do not share others' private info without permission.
  • Academic resources must be shared with proper credit.
  • Admins may remove content that violates campus guidelines.

By using DIU Nexus you agree to these terms. Updated 2026.

Help Center

Login issue? Use your DIU email + password. Forgot? Contact admin.

How to post? After login, go to Home → Create Post (photo/video/text).

Blood/Housing/Marketplace? Use the Student Corner in left sidebar.

Report a problem? Use the Flag icon on any post — reports go directly to Admin.
